*How to read these items.* A **different reading** is evidence that one misreading could not have produced on its own — a check that can fail, a verifier's verdict, a contract where the claim was not in the agent's context, or you; the **same reading** is the agent judging its own work with the claim already in front of it. The **certainty** word (very-low, low, moderate, high) sums up what stands behind a claim, followed by the named reasons it was raised or lowered, each of which you can dispute; the **observer's forecast** is the probability the observer wrote down, before any checking, that you would adopt the claim, kept as the guess it was and never scored. None of these figures asks anything of you — only the line **What is asked** does. Statements and situations are copied word for word, references and all: M-16 is the map entry for a review batch (review-due), B-001 is the first review batch, and a number such as C-027 is the kit's filing number for a recorded correction — nothing here asks you to look one up.

Items that carry a learning have two lines above the proposed disposition that are yours to fill first: *Verdict before evidence*, your own read of the statement before you see what the agent recommends, and *Against*, your one-line answer to "assume this is wrong — why?".
