# Review batch B-001 — 2026-09-22

*How to read these items.* Some items carry counts of how a claim has been confirmed or contradicted, split by
**a different reading** and **the same reading**: a different reading is evidence a single misreading could not
have produced on its own — a check that can fail, a verifier's verdict, a contract where the claim was not in
the agent's context, or you — while the same reading is the agent judging its own work with the claim already
in front of it. The **certainty** word (very-low, low, moderate, high) is a graded summary of what stands behind
a claim, followed by the named reasons it was raised or lowered, each of which you can dispute. The **observer's
forecast** is the probability the observer wrote down, before any checking, that you would adopt the claim; it is
kept as the guess it was and is never scored. None of these figures asks anything of you — only the line
**What is asked** does.

Statements and situations are copied word for word, references and all: where one carries a filing number such
as C-002, O-014 or P-001, that is the kit's own number for a recorded correction, observation or precedent, and
nothing in this batch asks you to look one up.

Items that carry a learning have two lines above the proposed disposition that are yours to fill first:
*Verdict before evidence*, your own reading of the statement before you see what the agent recommends, and
*Against*, your one-line answer to "assume this is wrong — why?".
